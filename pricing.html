Questions.

What happens to my entries if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. The book keeps working on the free plan: one book, three people, unlimited entries. The paid features stop, and you can export everything first.

Is any of this uploaded?

No. Every entry is written to this browser's local storage. That is why there is no server to pay for — and why clearing site data clears the book.

How do I get more people into one book?

The key is the invitation. Read the six digits out; anyone who types them opens the same list. The plan only sets how many people can hold a key at once.

Can two people be in the same entry?

One entry is one date, one type, one category, one amount. If two people paid, write two lines and a note on each; the balance adds them either way.

Can I actually buy a plan?

No. This is a student project and the pricing page is a design exercise: there is no checkout and nothing takes a payment. The free plan is the real one.
