Tell it to

Drag to move it · click to poke it · double-click to play · scroll over it to resize · right-click for more. These work on any page, not just this one.

What it does

  • Follows the site — the same pet, the same spot, on all seven pages.
  • Wanders — every few seconds it picks a spot nearby and hops over, facing the way it goes.
  • Sleeps — after about a minute without a poke it curls up; any touch wakes it up.
  • Reacts — a poke plays the cheer animation and a line in the bubble.
  • Remembers — where it was and how big it got stay in this browser (deskPet).

Four sprite sheets, 24 + 12 + 12 + 12 frames, drawn on a canvas at the frame rate each animation wants. With prefers-reduced-motion it stays put and only changes pose.
