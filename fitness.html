Fitness

  • Cardio, by the book. 150–300 minutes of moderate work a week, or 75–150 vigorous — that is the range the WHO guidance lands on, and it is enough for health before it is enough for fat loss.
  • Progressive overload. Add a little weight, a rep, or a cleaner pause each week. If the numbers do not move for three weeks, the training has become a ritual.
  • Intensity you can name. Strength sets end at RPE 7–8: two or three reps left in the tank, form still good. Anything past that eats recovery you need next week.
  • Start smaller than feels serious. Four weeks of two sessions a week beats one heroic week and a month of nothing.

Eating 饮食指南

A deficit decides whether you lose weight; protein and fibre decide what you lose and how hungry you are while losing it.

  • Protein first. 1.6–2.2 g per kg a day, and lean towards the top of that range while in a deficit — the higher intake is what protects muscle when calories are low (meta-analysis, 2024).
  • Fibre to 25–35 g. Two fists of vegetables, one piece of fruit, whole grains instead of white ones. It is the cheapest appetite control there is.
  • The hand method, when you cannot weigh food. One palm of protein, one cupped hand of carbs, two fists of vegetables, one thumb of fat — per meal.
  • Cut the drinks first. Milk tea, juice and alcohol are the easiest 300–500 kcal to remove because they never made you feel full.
  • Do not go under the floor. Below roughly 1200 kcal (women) or 1500 (men) a day, without supervision, you are gambling with muscle, hormones and hair.

Fitness habits 健身指南

The five movements cover the whole body. Learn them once and every program afterwards is a variation.

  • Squat, hinge, push, pull, carry. If a week contains all five, nothing has been left out.
  • Warm up in five minutes. Two minutes of easy movement, then the first exercise with an empty bar or bodyweight. Stretching before lifting does not prevent injury; warming up does.
  • Measure the trend, not the day. Weight fluctuates 1–2 kg with salt, carbs and sleep. Compare weekly averages, and add the waist tape — it moves when the scale sulks.
  • Sleep is part of the program. 7–9 hours. Short sleep raises appetite and lowers the willpower you are counting on at 22:00.
  • When it stalls, check adherence first. Three weeks of a real plateau usually means portions drifted, not that the plan is broken. Then add 1–2k steps before cutting food again.
  • Deload every 6–8 weeks. One lighter week keeps joints and motivation intact; it costs nothing in fat loss because the deficit is doing that work.

Before you start

This is general information, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor first if any of these applies: a BMI at or above 30, pregnancy, diabetes or blood-pressure medication, a history of disordered eating, or an injury that changes how you can move. Stop and get help if you feel faint, dizzy, or if food starts to feel like a rule you are afraid to break.

Sources: WHO physical activity guidance, protein intake and muscle retention (2024), Endotext: dietary treatment of obesity.
